About me

I have always enjoyed making apps that are a little useful and a little unnecessary. You need to make dinner and have no idea what to eat? That's what Pantry Panic is for! You had an amazing wine with your friend and want to save the information about it somewhere? Then SipLog is for you! Have no idea what to wear in the morning? Well, I have good news for you! My closet app will AI generate an outfit for you. I find small problems and create software to make that problem a little less annoying.

Before software, I spent about 10 years working in hospitality, including Michelin starred restaurants. That is where I learnt to read the room, stay composed under a lot of pressure and treat every small problem as if it matters, because it does. These are the same instincts and attention to details I bring to code. And I love both sides, the logic and the craft.

Projects

01

Pantry Panic

I can barely cook, so I turned to HelloFresh to learn. It got expensive fast, so I decided to build something of my own instead: a goofy, cheerful app that helps me push through the panic of what to eat and puts a smile on my face while doing it. Pantry Panic can generate an AI recipe for just tonight's dinner or plan out my entire week, then writes the shopping list and walks me through every recipe.

Built using: Next.js, TypeScript, Expo / React Native, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Claude API, GitHub Actions

  • Recipe library with AI suggestions by mood or ingredients on hand, full step-by-step instructions included
  • Favorites and tags to organize saved recipes
  • Weekly planner with breakfast, lunch, and dinner slots
  • Auto-generated shopping list that combines duplicate ingredients across the week
  • Private accounts with row-level security protecting each user's recipes, plan, and list
